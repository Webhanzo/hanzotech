# **App Name**: HanzoTech

## Core Features:

- Product Catalog: Display a curated list of tech products, including laptops and phones, with details like images, descriptions, prices, and condition (new/used). Products are filterable by category and price range, and sortable by price.
- Shopping Cart: Allow users to add products to a virtual shopping cart, view cart items, and proceed to checkout.
- Homepage Showcase: Enable users to browse featured products and promotional banners on the homepage, encouraging purchases.
- Contact Form: Provide a contact form for users to send inquiries. Display a modern and accessible Contact Us page.
- About us Section: Provide an About Us section that lets the store present the vision to its customers.
- AI Product Recommendations: AI-powered product recommendations on the product details page, offering alternatives and complementary items to enhance the shopping experience. An AI tool will examine the current product details to reason about similar items that can be added to its list.
- Responsive Layout: Implement a static layout with a responsive design, ensuring optimal viewing experience across various devices (desktops, tablets, and smartphones).

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: A muted crimson (#E63946), echoing the current branding and creating a sense of sophisticated energy.
- Background color: A light grey (#F4F4F4), providing a clean and modern backdrop that ensures product images stand out.
- Accent color: A deep teal (#2A9D8F), used sparingly for calls to action and interactive elements, complementing the primary color and adding a touch of freshness.
- Headline font: 'Space Grotesk', sans-serif, for a modern and technological feel that reflects the nature of the products.
- Body font: 'Inter', sans-serif, for a clean, readable, and neutral look, ensuring comfortable reading experience.
- Use a set of Font Awesome icons, maintaining consistency with the current site for a familiar user experience. Icons should be used sparingly to enhance, not overwhelm, the design.
- Employ a grid-based layout for product listings, ensuring a clean and organized presentation. Utilize whitespace effectively to create a sense of openness and prevent visual clutter.
